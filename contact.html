Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How long does a typical construction project take?

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. A typical residential renovation takes 2-4 weeks, while new construction can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines during the consultation phase.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, we provide free, no-obligation estimates for all potential projects. Contact us to schedule a consultation and receive a detailed quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured. All our technicians are certified professionals, and we maintain comprehensive liability insurance for all projects.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater metropolitan area and surrounding regions. Contact us to confirm we can service your specific location.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for larger projects. We discuss payment options during the consultation and work with you to find a solution that fits your budget.

What warranties do you offer?

We stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties. Labor warranties typically range from 1-5 years, and we pass through manufacturer warranties on all materials used.
